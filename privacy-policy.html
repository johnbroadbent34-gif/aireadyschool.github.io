Privacy Policy

How AI Ready Schools collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: September 2025  ·  This policy applies to aireadyschool.co.uk
This privacy policy explains how AI Ready Schools ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, sign up for our mailing list, download resources, or get in touch with us. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

AI Ready Schools is a content and guidance platform for school leaders, operated by [Your Full Name], a deputy headteacher based in the United Kingdom. The website is published at aireadyschool.co.uk.

For data protection purposes, [Your Full Name] is the data controller. If you have any questions about how we handle your data, please use the contact details at the bottom of this page.

2. What information we collect

When you sign up for our mailing list or download the free guide

We collect your first name, email address, and (optionally) your role in education. This is collected via our email signup form, which is processed by Mailchimp.

When you contact us

We collect your name, email address, role, and the content of your message when you submit the contact form on our website.

When you visit our website

Like most websites, we collect certain technical data automatically — including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This is collected via cookies and analytics tools (see section 5).

We do not collect any sensitive personal data (such as health information, ethnicity, or financial details). We never collect or store pupil data of any kind.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To send you the free guide you requested and any follow-up emails you have opted into
  • To send our newsletter — articles, resources, and guidance for school leaders (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To respond to your contact form messages directly and personally
  • To understand how our website is used so we can improve content and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations where required

We will never sell your personal data to third parties. We will never use your data for advertising purposes. We will never pass your data to commercial organisations without your explicit consent.

4. Our lawful basis for processing

Under UK GDPR we must have a lawful basis for processing your personal data. The basis we rely on depends on the context:

  • Consent — when you sign up to our mailing list or download a resource, you are giving us consent to contact you. You can withdraw this consent at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Legitimate interests — for website analytics and improving our content, we rely on our legitimate interest in running an effective website for school leaders.
  • Contract — where we are responding to a direct request you have made (such as a contact form message).

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to help the site function and to understand how visitors use it.

Essential cookies

These are necessary for the website to work and cannot be turned off. They do not store personally identifiable information.

Analytics cookies

We use anonymised analytics (via Framer's built-in analytics or Google Analytics) to understand which pages are visited and how people navigate the site. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual visitors.

Third-party cookies

If you interact with an embedded form (such as our Mailchimp signup form), third-party cookies may be set by those services. Please refer to Mailchimp's privacy policy for details.

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the site.

6. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate our website and communicate with subscribers. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Framer — website hosting and analytics (framer.com/legal/privacy-statement)
  • Mailchimp (Intuit) — email list management and newsletter delivery (mailchimp.com/legal/privacy)
  • Google Drive — hosting downloadable resources such as the free guide PDF

We have chosen these services carefully and only share the minimum data necessary for them to function.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Email subscribers — we retain your details for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, your data is removed from our active list within 30 days.
  • Contact form messages — retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Website analytics — anonymised analytics data is retained for up to 26 months.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to access
You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to rectification
You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
Right to erasure
You can ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
Right to object
You can object to us processing your data for marketing purposes.
Right to restrict processing
You can ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
Right to withdraw consent
Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.

9. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our website is served over HTTPS. Access to subscriber data via Mailchimp is protected by two-factor authentication.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. If you have concerns about a specific data security issue, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time — for example, if we add new features to the site or if legal requirements change. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
