What's inside
Five areas every school leader needs to assess
01
Staff Awareness & Understanding
Do your staff genuinely understand what AI is, what it isn't, and the ethical implications of using it in a school context?
02
Governance & Policy Foundations
Is there a written, governor-approved AI policy in place — one that references KCSIE 2025 and the DfE product safety guidance?
03
Tool Selection & Data Safety
Are your approved AI tools within your data tenancy? Has your DPO reviewed them? Do staff know which tools are — and aren't — permitted?
04
CPD & Training Readiness
Has training been tailored by role? Do staff feel confident rather than fearful? Is there a plan to teach pupils about safe AI use?
05
Ongoing Oversight & Review
Is AI use reviewed regularly by SLT? Is there a named AI lead? Can staff raise concerns safely? Is the policy kept up to date?

Plus a scoring guide
